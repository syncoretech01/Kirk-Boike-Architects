Every house starts with the land. We walk the site before drawing a line, then design the home and engineer its structure under one roof. One studio, one set of drawings, one person answering the phone.

What we do

Site design

Grading, drainage, driveway, septic and setbacks are designed with the house, not after it. The plan follows the slope and the sun.

Structural design

Framing, beams and the lateral system are engineered in the same room the plans are drawn in. Long spans and big glass without guesswork.

Home design

Plans, elevations, materials and details for new-build custom homes and small projects. Built to be lived in for decades in Peninsula weather.

Built for Peninsula weather

Salt air, sideways rain and long dry summers. The material list is short and every item on it has been on a house here for a decade or more.
